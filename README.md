# 🎭 Nocturne Ledger — Solo Combat Companion for Limbus Company

![status](https://img.shields.io/badge/status-active-6b4cff?style=flat-square) ![platform](https://img.shields.io/badge/platform-desktop%20%7C%20steam%20deck-1f2937?style=flat-square) ![interface](https://img.shields.io/badge/interface-responsive-0ea5e9?style=flat-square) ![languages](https://img.shields.io/badge/languages-multilingual-22c55e?style=flat-square) ![support](https://img.shields.io/badge/support-24%2F7-f59e0b?style=flat-square) ![license](https://img.shields.io/badge/license-MIT-a855f7?style=flat-square) ![year](https://img.shields.io/badge/release-2026-111827?style=flat-square)

> A nimble tactical ledger for solo expeditions through the City — built for players who want their runs to feel like a well-rehearsed ballet instead of a blindfolded coin toss.

Nocturne Ledger is an independent companion overlay and tuning suite for **Limbus Company**, designed around the quiet pleasure of a solo run that finally behaves the way you always imagined it could. Where a noisy pile of toggles would overwhelm you, Nocturne Ledger arranges everything into a calm, legible panel: composure management, sinner tuning, defensive posture, and damage pacing — all within arm's reach. Think of it less as a toolbox and more as a conductor's baton for the chaos of mirror dungeon dives and story stages alike.

The name says it: a ledger. Every battle is a set of entries, and Nocturne Ledger helps you write the ones you actually want to remember. Raised composure when a clash goes wrong. Softer incoming hits when a sinner is one stagger away from collapse. A little extra bite in your damage curve when the enemy refuses to cooperate. Nothing theatrical — just consistency, applied with taste.

---

## 🌌 What Nocturne Ledger Actually Is

Nocturne Ledger began as a personal notebook of frustrations. A solo player wanted their favorite IDs to feel the way they do in carefully planned team compositions, without permanently dragging four other characters along just to soak up punishment. So the ledger grew: a soft-spoken desktop application that reads your combat state, presents a clean dashboard, and lets you bias a run toward the outcome you were already aiming for. It is not a shortcut past the skill ceiling — it is a handrail beside it.

Everything inside the ledger is organized around one question: *does this make a solo run feel more like you, and less like a dice roll?* Features that fail that test get trimmed. Features that pass it get polished until they hum.

If you have ever watched a perfect clash chain fall apart because one unstable ego roll flipped the wrong way, this was built for you. If you have ever wished a sinner could catch their breath without retreating from the fight entirely, this was built for you too.

## ⚙️ Core Capabilities

A quick ledger index of what the companion offers. Each item is described in plain terms — no obfuscated jargon, no mystery toggles.

### 🧠 Composure & Sanity Stewardship
- **Stabilized Composure Floor** — bias a minimum sanity state so a solo sinner does not spiral into a madness chain after a single unfortunate clash.
- **Post-Clash Recovery Assist** — restores a small amount of composure after taking a hit, simulating the rhythm of a supporting ally who never actually appears on the field.
- **Ego-Roll Comfort** — softly favors sane outcomes on unstable rolls without claiming certainty, keeping the tension of the roll intact.
- **Low-Sanity Visual Calm** — the ledger panel fades its warning highlights when sanity is intentionally kept low, so your screen does not scream at you for the state you chose.

### 🛡️ Defensive Posture Tuning
- **Stagger Buffer** — adds a gentle cushion before a stagger threshold triggers, buying a solo sinner one more beat to act.
- **Bleed & Tremor Mitigation Bias** — reduces the tick pressure of status-based attrition so long fights remain winnable without turning trivial.
- **Guard Frame Reinforcement** — nudges defensive skills to feel slightly more reliable when the deck draws are unkind.
- **Resurrection Room** — around the collapse point, gives the player a widening window to stabilize before the run ends.

### ⚔️ Damage Pacing & Rhythm
- **Baseline Damage Bias** — a subtle, hand-tuned adjustment to outgoing hit values that keeps a solo run's damage curve smooth rather than spiky.
- **Clash Advantage Nudge** — slightly prefilters the clash math in your favor, preserving the sensation of a fair duel.
- **Ego Burst Timers** — visual countdown overlays for ego cooldowns, so burst damage feels like an orchestrated crescendo, not a guess.
- **Finisher Read** — a calm indicator of when a target is within a damage range that would end the encounter, letting you decide when to close the ledger.

### 🧬 Sinner Configuration
- **Per-Sinner Presets** — save distinct tuning profiles for each favorite identity, so switching from a Yi Sang run to a Faust run is one click, not ten minutes.
- **Stat Curve Editor** — adjust small, bounded offsets to core statistics without turning your sinner into an unrecognizable anomaly.
- **Skill Weight Recall** — remembers which skills you prefer to see come up and lightly prefers them in the rotation.
- **Loadout Snapshotting** — capture a full configuration (composure, defense, damage) and reload it later for repeatable difficulty curves.

### 🖥️ Interface & Comfort Layer
- **Responsive Panel Design** — adapts cleanly from ultrawide monitors to a Steam Deck display to a compact laptop screen without shuffling your layout.
- **Multilingual Interface** — a language selector covering a broad set of locales, so the ledger speaks your language, not just one.
- **Keyboard-First Flow** — every toggle is reachable without leaving the game window; a hotkey overlay doubles as a cheat sheet.
- **Session Memory** — the ledger reopens exactly where you left it, down to scroll position and last-selected profile.
- **Readable Theme System** — high-contrast, low-glare, and colorblind-conscious palettes that stay legible under a long session.
- **Quiet Mode** — collapses to a single slim bar when all you need is a glance, so the City stays the star of the screen.

## 🎨 Why Nocturne Ledger Feels Different

Most companion tools aim for maximum features per square inch. Nocturne Ledger aims for maximum *fit*. The design premise is that a solo run is an act of authorship — you are choosing to bring one character into a room full of consequences, and the tool you use should honor that choice with restraint.

Three principles guide the entire repository:

1. **Quiet defaults.** Everything ships off. You turn on precisely the entries you want, and the ledger records them. No surprise behavior, no secret settings doing work behind your back.
2. **Reversible tuning.** Every adjustment sits inside a conservative range and every change can be undone with a single profile reload. If a value makes the run feel wrong, you are one click from sanity.
3. **Readable state.** At any moment you can look at the ledger and answer "what is currently influencing my run?" in under five seconds. No hidden accumulation, no invisible modifiers.

The tone of the project matters as much as the code. The community around Nocturne Ledger trades configuration files the way chefs trade recipes — with notes, with reasoning, with a story about the run that inspired the change. The repository's discussion space is a cooking show for tactical tinkering.

## 🚀 Getting Started in a Few Minutes

There is nothing complicated about the first launch. The ledger is designed so a new user is inside a tuned solo run before the coffee cools.

1. Acquire the current release package for your platform. Choose the build that matches your operating system and unpack it into a folder you will remember.
2. Run the launcher. On first start, the ledger walks you through a short setup: pick your display mode, pick your language, pick your theme. That is the entire ceremony.
3. Launch Limbus Company as you normally would. The ledger detects the running session and attaches itself without needing to be opened first next time.
4. Open the profile panel and start with the "Gentle Solo" preset. It is deliberately mild — a good baseline before you get opinionated.
5. Adjust one thing at a time. Turn on composure support, run a fight, watch how it feels. The ledger is far more satisfying when you tune it like an instrument rather than set it like an alarm.

For users on handheld hardware, a dedicated compact layout is available in the interface settings. For users on very large displays, the ledger panel supports an expanded mode with pinned stat readouts.

## 🔧 Configuration Deep Dive

The config surface is deliberately wide but shallow — many options, each with a narrow range of behavior. This keeps the ledger predictable while still offering the granularity that tinkerers crave.

### Profile Files
Each profile is a small, human-readable document. You can duplicate a profile, rename it, and share it with a friend. Profiles are the unit of community exchange: "try my stagger-safe Yi Sang build" is a link you can send.

### Preset Families
The ledger ships with several families of presets, each representing a philosophy rather than a fixed configuration:
- **Gentle Solo** — minimal assistance, maximum tension preserved.
- **Steady Diver** — balanced support for long mirror dungeon chains.
- **Boss Focus** — defensive-biased tuning for the heavy names.
- **Speed Ledger** — offense-biased tuning for players who want fast, decisive runs.
- **Attrition Ward** — status-resistance-biased tuning for long, grind-heavy fights.

### Numeric Bounds and Safety
All tunable values are clamped to conservative bounds at the engine level. Even if a profile document is edited by hand, the ledger refuses to leave the safe envelope. This is intentional: it makes the tool trustworthy and keeps the community from accidentally cooking up a run that breaks the game's pacing outright.

### Backup and Versioning
The ledger keeps rolling backups of the last several profiles. If you experiment and regret it, restoring a prior state is a quick action in the profile menu. You will never lose a beloved setup to a bad experiment.

## 🧭 Solo Run Playbook

A brief guide to how experienced ledger users shape a run from start to finish.

**Before the dive.** Choose a preset that matches the stage type. Boss stages favor the Boss Focus family; long exploration favors Steady Diver. Open the ledger's readout panel and confirm the active profile is what you expect. A 10-second check prevents a 20-minute regret.

**Early fights.** Leave damage bias near baseline. Early fights are where you learn the rhythm of the stage — do not tune them away. Use composure support lightly if you notice sanity dropping faster than you planned.

**Mid-stage attrition.** This is where stagger buffers and status mitigation earn their keep. If a specific status is chewing through your sinner, bump its mitigation bias up one notch and watch. Do not stack multiple mitigations at once; the ledger is meant to be sipped, not gulped.

**Boss approach.** Switch to the Boss Focus family and enable defensive reinforcements. Keep your damage bias conservative — you want the boss fight to still feel earned. If you are chasing a specific outcome (a clean no-stagger clear, for instance), this is where a saved loadout snapshot pays off.

**Post-run review.** The ledger keeps a light history of what toggles were active per session. Before you close the game, glance at it. The best configurations are discovered by looking back at which toggles were on during your best runs.

## 📱 Interface & Responsive Design

The ledger's interface has been rebuilt twice around a simple rule: the panel must never ask you to look away from the action for more than a breath.

- **Adaptive layout zones** — panels rearrange themselves into a vertical stack on narrow screens and a horizontal dashboard on wide ones.
- **Glance-friendly typography** — stat readouts use a carefully tuned typeface that remains legible at small sizes and high DPI.
- **Colorblind-conscious palette** — every state indicator (good, warning, critical) carries a shape or pattern, so color is never the only signal.
- **Dark-first design** — the default theme is a low-glare dark surface tuned for long evening sessions.
- **Handheld mode** — the Steam Deck and comparable devices get a dedicated touch-friendly layout with larger hit targets.

Nocturne Ledger believes interface comfort is a feature, not a polish pass. You will notice the difference within the first hour.

## 🌍 Multilingual Support

The ledger speaks many languages because a solo run is not just a mechanical challenge — it is a story, and stories are best read in your native tongue.

- Runtime language switch without restarting.
- Community-maintained translation files, so corrections arrive quickly.
- Fallback logic that gracefully shows a default locale string if a translation entry is missing, rather than a raw identifier.
- Right-to-left layout support for the locales that need it.
- Every user-facing string lives in a single localization directory, which makes contributing a translation approachable for anyone.

If you want to see the ledger in your language, the localization folder is one of the most welcoming places to start contributing.

A tool used at 3 AM on a Sunday deserves support that does not clock out at 5 PM on a Friday.

- **Round-the-clock issue triage** — community maintainers rotate coverage so a broken build rarely stays broken until morning.

## 🧪 Testing and Quality Signals

Every release passes through a sequence of checks before it reaches users.

- **Deterministic engine tests** — the tuning engine is tested against synthetic combat states to ensure bounded behavior.
- **Profile round-trip tests** — every shipped preset is loaded, saved, reloaded, and diffed to catch silent regressions.
- **Cross-platform smoke tests** — builds are exercised on representative machines for each supported family of hardware.
- **Telemetry-free by design** — the ledger does not phone home. Quality signals come from community reports, not silent data collection.
- **Reproducible builds** — anyone can rebuild the exact binary from a tagged release tag, which keeps the trust chain short.

The repository prizes reproducibility over velocity. A slower release that everyone can verify is worth more than a faster one nobody can.

## 🔐 Privacy and Safety Posture

Nocturne Ledger treats the user's machine as the user's own.

- No background account creation.
- No silent updates — you see a changelog before you update, always.
- No remote configuration of behavior — the ledger decides nothing about your run without your profile saying so.
- No third-party ad or analytics payloads embedded in builds.
- Everything runs locally; the ledger does not need the network to function.

This is a tool for your own solo enjoyment, on your own hardware, governed by your own profile files. The repository aims to keep it that way by design.

## 🤝 Contributing

Contributions of every size are welcome: a corrected translation string, a new preset family, a bug report with a reproduction path, a documentation clarification, or a small engine improvement.

- **Bug reports** — include your platform, ledger version, the active profile name, and a description of expected versus observed behavior.
